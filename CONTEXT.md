# BeakSpeak Learning

BeakSpeak teaches bird identification through an ordered lesson path and continuing review. These terms describe the learner-visible progression model shared by the web and mobile apps.

## Language

**Guided Path**:
The ordered sequence of bird lessons. A lesson normally opens after the preceding lesson is complete, and active relearning can temporarily recommend practice before continuing.
_Avoid_: Course, curriculum

**Introduced Species**:
A bird that has entered the learner's progress record and is eligible to appear in reviews. Introduction does not imply demonstrated mastery.
_Avoid_: Learned bird, mastered bird

**Lesson Complete**:
A lesson whose species are all introduced. Completion records progression through the Guided Path; it does not imply that every bird has reached mastery.
_Avoid_: Mastered lesson, passed lesson

**Skip Ahead**:
The learner-confirmed action that opens a locked future lesson. Species in skipped lessons become Introduced Species, so those lessons become complete and their birds can appear in reviews.
_Avoid_: Fast-forward, query-string unlock
