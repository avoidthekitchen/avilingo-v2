BeakSpeak privacy

Effective October 9, 2026. This policy covers the BeakSpeak iPhone app and the web demo at beakspeak.app/beakspeak/.

BeakSpeak is a free bird-song learning app created by Jason Wu and Armand Ian Lucas. It has no accounts, advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-device tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

Learning progress stays on your device

BeakSpeak saves which birds you have been introduced to, review scheduling and results, and quiz confusion history locally. The iPhone app uses a local database; the web demo uses your browser's storage. These learning records are not sent to us or synchronized between devices or between the app and demo.

The iPhone database is eligible for device backups according to your Apple backup settings. BeakSpeak does not operate a cloud backup service. Browser storage may be cleared by your browser or its settings.

Photos, sound, and external websites

The iPhone app bundles its bird photos, recordings, and learning content. While you learn, it makes no network requests, so lessons, quizzes, and reviews work without a connection. Earlier TestFlight beta builds loaded bird photos from Wikimedia servers, which disclosed ordinary network information such as your IP address and the requested photo to Wikimedia under its privacy policy. Updating to the current build removes those requests.

The web demo and these pages are served through Cloudflare. Unlike the installed app, the demo downloads its learning content, photos, and recordings from beakspeak.app and needs a connection. It does not load photos from Wikimedia or any other third party. Website requests disclose ordinary network information to the hosting provider; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. BeakSpeak does not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

Source, license, and support links open external services. Those services handle requests under their own policies. BeakSpeak does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or device-location access and does not record your surroundings.

Support and Apple services

If you choose to contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to respond and investigate your report. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence through the support contact; we delete it when it is no longer needed for support, except where retention is required by law.

Apple handles App Store distribution and device services under its own policies. For TestFlight testers, Apple makes tester activity, device and build information, crash reports, and submitted feedback available to the developer. BeakSpeak includes no third-party crash-reporting SDK. See Apple's privacy policy.

Resetting your data

In BeakSpeak, open Progress, choose Reset All Progress, and confirm Yes, Reset. This deletes local learning records and quiz confusion history. It does not delete copies in existing device backups, Apple-held feedback, or support messages. Clearing the demo's browser site data also removes its local progress. There is no account to delete.

Questions and policy changes

For privacy questions or support-message deletion, email support@beakspeak.app. We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change.
