BeakSpeak support

Need help learning bird songs, found a recording or identification problem, or have a privacy question? Email support@beakspeak.app. No BeakSpeak account is needed.

Include your app version (shown in About), iPhone model and iOS version, or browser if using the demo. Tell us what you tried and what happened. Screenshots are optional; please remove any personal information before sending them. We use your message to respond and investigate; see our privacy policy.

Getting started

Open Learn and start Lesson 1. Listen to each bird's songs and calls, move through the cards with Next, and take the introductory quiz. Then use Quiz for spaced repetition reviews and Progress to see introduced birds and upcoming reviews. BeakSpeak currently teaches 15 Seattle-area birds in five lessons.

I cannot hear a sound

Check your media volume and connected headphones or speakers. Tap the play control to play or replay a clip. If automatic playback is blocked, choose Tap to play sound. Playback stops when the app is backgrounded or your phone locks; return to the app and tap play again.

A photo is missing

Photos load from Wikimedia and need internet access. If a photo cannot load, BeakSpeak shows a bird illustration and you can continue. Recordings and learning content are bundled in the iPhone app. The web demo downloads its content; complete offline operation is not supported.

Where is my progress?

Progress is saved locally. It does not sync between devices or between the iPhone app and web demo. The iPhone database is eligible for device backups according to your settings. Clearing browser site data removes demo progress. After force-quitting, the app returns to the lesson list rather than restoring an exact card or question.

How do I reset?

Open Progress, choose Reset All Progress, and confirm Yes, Reset. This deletes local learning progress and quiz confusion history. Only reset if you want to start again; there is no BeakSpeak account or cloud save to restore it from.

Sources and credits

About lists recording and photo sources and licenses. To report a content problem, include the bird name and whether you were listening to its song or call.
