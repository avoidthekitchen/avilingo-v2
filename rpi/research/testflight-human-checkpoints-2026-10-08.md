# Human checkpoints for internal TestFlight issue #31

Research date: 2026-10-08. Apple and SQLCipher vendor primary documentation checked; this note does not establish the state of the owner's private Apple accounts. The owner reports paid Apple Developer Program enrollment is complete and has confirmed team `33M9E5MEJ9`. Subsequent release preparation is tracked in [the internal beta record](../../docs/testflight-beta.md).

## Current local preparation

The accompanying repository audit found BeakSpeak / `com.unformedideas.beakspeak` already configured consistently; version 2.0.0, build 1; iPhone portrait; iOS 18.4 deployment target; and an opaque 1024-pixel icon. Signing team `33M9E5MEJ9` is configured but still needs confirmation against the paid membership. The accepted Capacitor ADR exists at `docs/adr/0001-adopt-capacitor-for-ios.md`, and the latest merged CI completed successfully, including iOS. Capacitor, Cordova, SQLCipher, and ZIPFoundation privacy manifests are present in a simulator build; the exact distribution archive still needs audit. No app telemetry was found, but live Wikimedia image requests and explicit external-source browser links exist. These findings do not establish a final “no data collected” declaration or a wholly offline app.

## Decisions needed before distribution

1. **Confirm the permanent app identity.** Recommended values are app name **BeakSpeak**, iOS platform, primary language **English (U.S.)**, explicit bundle ID **`com.unformedideas.beakspeak`**, and internal SKU **`beakspeak-ios`**. Name and identifier availability must be checked in the owner's account. Apple requires an app record before upload; SKU cannot change after creation and bundle ID cannot change after uploading a build. Suggested initial marketing version **2.0.0**, build **1**, is a project recommendation subject to existing uploads. [Create an app record](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-a-new-app/), [App information reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information)

2. **Confirm which Apple membership owns the app.** Sign in to the paid account, open **Membership details**, and verify its Team ID against the configured `33M9E5MEJ9`; the permanent identifier and signing must belong to that membership. If enrollment is individual, the seller/developer name is the owner's legal name; a custom brand developer name is available only for an organization with a qualifying registered trade name. This is an ownership consequence, not a requirement to upgrade enrollment for an internal beta. [Find Team ID](https://developer.apple.com/help/glossary/team-id/), [Individual seller name](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/), [Developer name](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/set-your-developer-name)

3. **Choose the second tester and authorize their invitations.** For the bird expert, collect first name, last name, and an invitation email, then grant **Marketing**, limited to BeakSpeak, without reports access. This grants real App Store Connect permissions; it is more than a tester-only invitation. Individual members can add up to 50 additional App Store Connect users, although those users do not join the Developer Program team. Marketing is eligible for internal testing. The expert accepts the App Store Connect invitation, then the TestFlight invitation; the first invitation expires after three days. The invitation address need not already be an Apple Account address. [Add users](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/add-and-edit-users/), [TestFlight roles](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/)

4. **Choose a monitored feedback email.** Codex can draft the beta description and What to Test text. Apple's additional beta-description/review-information requirements apply to external testing; internal-only testers do not require that additional information. Apple nevertheless includes What to Test in the internal-group build flow, and issue #31 may require richer beta information independently. The feedback address is also the reply-to address on invitations. [Internal tester definition](https://developer.apple.com/help/glossary/internal-testers/), [Provide test information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/provide-test-information/), [Internal group workflow](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/add-internal-testers/)

## Human actions when prompted

- **Authenticate and accept agreements.** Sign in to App Store Connect and Xcode, handle two-factor authentication, and personally review any blocking agreement. Apple assigns agreement acceptance to the Account Holder. Paid Apps Agreement, banking, and tax setup are tied to paid apps or in-app purchases; do not assume these are prerequisites for this free beta. [App Store Connect workflow](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/get-started/app-store-connect-workflow), [Agreements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/sign-and-update-agreements/)
- **Make paid signing available locally.** In Xcode Settings → Accounts, use the paid membership, then select that team with automatic signing for the app. Xcode can manage signing identities. Codex can prepare the project, register/check the explicit identifier using authorized access, and troubleshoot archive validation; login and Keychain prompts may still need the owner. No password, 2FA code, or private key needs to be pasted into chat. [Signing identities](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/sharing-your-teams-signing-certificates), [Explicit App ID](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/register-an-app-id)
- **Confirm export-compliance answers after the archive audit.** Apple considers encryption an app uses, accesses, or **contains**. SQLCipher being linked while database encryption is disabled does not by itself justify answering “no encryption” or setting `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption=NO`. Audit the actual shipping archive, describe included cryptography and its use, follow Apple's questionnaire, and record the owner's determination. The SQLCipher vendor confirms strong cryptography and application developers' export responsibilities, but does not settle this app's disabled-mode classification. If necessary, resolve classification with Apple rather than guessing. Missing Compliance can block testing. [Export-compliance scope](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/overview-of-export-compliance/), [Beta compliance workflow](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/provide-export-compliance-information-for-beta-builds/), [SQLCipher export guidance](https://www.zetetic.net/sqlcipher/export-compliance/)
- **Install and exercise the distributed build.** Both testers install Apple's TestFlight app, accept invitations, and install/launch BeakSpeak. A human must report learner-visible results on the physical iPhone. Codex can supply the short procedure and inspect installation/session/crash/feedback metrics. TestFlight automatically shares crash logs; it supports screenshot feedback without adding an analytics SDK. [TestFlight workflow](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview/), [Build metrics](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/view-build-status-and-metrics)

## Implementation choices Codex can make

- Use a **normal App Store Connect upload** and distribute it to an internal group. An upload marked **TestFlight Internal Only** cannot later be used for external testing or customers. Recommended group: **Internal Beta**, with manual build assignment initially. Builds expire after 90 days. [Internal distribution](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/add-internal-testers/)
- Prepare identity/version changes, icons, privacy-manifest/dependency audit, native packaging, archive/export tooling, beta text, validation, and the install checklist before portal work.
- Check the current toolchain: uploads require **Xcode 26+ with iOS 26 SDK+**; since September 9, 2026, uploaded iOS apps must target iOS 13+. This concerns build tooling and deployment target, not a requirement that testers run iOS 26. [Current upload requirements](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/)
- API automation is optional. The Account Holder must request API access; private keys download only once. Team keys apply across all apps, while individual keys follow user access and cannot call provisioning endpoints. First-run Xcode/portal authentication is sufficient to proceed without designing CI credentials now. [App Store Connect API](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/get-started/app-store-connect-api/), [API key capabilities](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/AppStoreConnectAPI/creating-api-keys-for-app-store-connect-api)
- Draft a truthful beta privacy note/policy covering local progress, remote media/source links, and TestFlight diagnostics. Apple broadly requires an iOS privacy policy URL, while its App Privacy help explicitly ties the data-handling questionnaire to App Store distribution. The beta localization API recommends a privacy policy for apps collecting user/device data. These sources do not establish that the full public-store privacy questionnaire is an internal-testing prerequisite; they also do not justify declaring a privacy policy unnecessary. The owner can review the concrete draft later. [App Privacy requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy/), [Beta privacy metadata](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreconnectapi/betaapplocalizationupdaterequest/data-data.dictionary/attributes-data.dictionary)

Completing local release preparation is distinct from completing distribution: issue #31 closure requires the processed build, actual tester installation evidence, and diagnostics verification. A later TestFlight update can verify SQLite preservation under issue #32; that separate test requires retaining the first beta's app data and installing the next build without uninstalling. The two-build update test is not a prerequisite for closing #31.
