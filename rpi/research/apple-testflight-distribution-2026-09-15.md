# Apple enrollment and internal TestFlight distribution for issue #31

Research date: 2026-09-15. Sources are Apple documentation only.

## Recommended order of operations

1. **Enroll the product owner as an individual.** Start at [Apple Developer Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/), sign in with the Apple Account that will permanently own the app, enable two-factor authentication, and make sure that account's first and last name are the owner's legal name. Apple verifies legal name, email, phone, and a physical address (not a P.O. box); after verification the owner accepts the license agreement and pays the annual fee (currently US$99, varying by region). For an individual membership, the legal personal name is displayed as the App Store seller name. A D-U-N-S number is not needed. Apple supports enrollment on the web and through the Apple Developer app, so the screens can differ slightly. [Apple enrollment requirements](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)

2. **Wait for membership activation, then accept the latest agreement.** Membership creates the owner's App Store Connect account. Sign in at [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/). Only the Account Holder initially has access. If App Store Connect shows an agreement banner, the Account Holder must accept it in **Business** before a new app record can be created. The separate Paid Apps Agreement plus tax and banking information is needed to sell paid apps or offer in-app purchases; Apple does not list it as a prerequisite for a free internal TestFlight build. [App Store Connect workflow](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/get-started/app-store-connect-workflow), [Add a new app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-a-new-app)

3. **Choose the permanent identity before registering anything.** Decide the customer-facing App Store name (2–30 characters) and a permanent reverse-DNS bundle ID. The existing repository value, `com.unformedideas.beakspeak.feasibility`, is explicitly disposable and should not be uploaded. A likely permanent candidate is `com.unformedideas.beakspeak`, but availability must be confirmed in the owner's account. Bundle IDs must match between Capacitor, the Xcode target, the registered App ID, the App Store Connect record, and the uploaded binary. The App Store Connect bundle ID cannot be changed after a build is uploaded, and the SKU cannot be changed after the app record is created. [App information reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information), [Preparing an app for distribution](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/preparing-your-app-for-distribution)

4. **Register the explicit App ID.** In the [Apple Developer account](https://developer.apple.com/account/), open **Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → Identifiers → + → App IDs → App**, enter a description, choose **Explicit App ID**, enter the permanent bundle ID, enable only capabilities the app actually uses, review, and register. Apple says the explicit ID must match the Xcode target. The Account Holder can do this. [Register an App ID](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/register-an-app-id)

5. **Create the App Store Connect app record.** In App Store Connect choose **Apps → + → New App**. Select **iOS**, enter the final name, primary language, registered bundle ID, and an internal SKU (for example `beakspeak-ios`; it is not customer-visible). Choose **Limited Access** if the bird expert should be restricted to this app; the Account Holder always sees all apps. The app record must exist before upload. [Add a new app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-a-new-app)

6. **Configure the distributable app in the repository/Xcode.** Replace the feasibility bundle ID consistently in `beakspeak/capacitor.config.ts` and the generated Xcode application target by using the project's normal Capacitor sync workflow. In Xcode, open the App project, select the App target, and:
   - under **Signing & Capabilities**, select the owner's team and use **Automatically manage signing**;
   - verify the final bundle ID, marketing version, unique incrementing build number, iOS deployment target, portrait-only orientation, app icons, launch presentation, and only the permissions/capabilities the app actually needs;
   - use a real distribution configuration, not the disposable feasibility identity.

   Xcode can create the required distribution certificate and provisioning profile automatically; Xcode 13 or later can cloud-sign from Organizer. [Signing workflow](https://help.apple.com/xcode/mac/current/en.lproj/dev60b6fbbc7.html), [Distribution signing options](https://help.apple.com/xcode/mac/current/en.lproj/devff5ececf8.html), [Cloud-managed certificates](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/certificates/cloud-managed-certificates)

7. **Archive and upload a normal App Store build.** In Xcode select the App scheme and a generic iOS device or physical device, then **Product → Archive**. In **Window → Organizer → Archives**, select the archive, optionally run **Validate App**, then choose **Distribute App → TestFlight & App Store** (or the equivalent custom **App Store Connect → Upload** path), keep automatic signing and symbol upload enabled, review the signing details, and upload. Do **not** choose **TestFlight Internal Only**: issue #31 requires a normal build that remains eligible for later external testing or App Store submission. Wait for Apple's processing email and for the build to appear under the app's TestFlight tab. Apple currently requires iOS uploads to be built with Xcode 26 or later. [Xcode distribution workflow](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/distributing-your-app-for-beta-testing-and-releases), [Upload builds](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-builds/upload-builds)

8. **Resolve build compliance prompts.** App Store Connect may ask whether the build uses encryption. Apple requires an export-compliance determination even when encryption comes only from the operating system. For an app that uses only exempt encryption such as OS-provided HTTPS, Apple documents setting `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` to `NO` in the app's `Info.plist` to avoid answering on every submission; verify all linked third-party libraries before making that declaration. Proprietary or non-exempt encryption needs documentation. [Export compliance overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/overview-of-export-compliance), [Complying with encryption export regulations](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/complying-with-encryption-export-regulations)

9. **Add the bird expert with least privilege.** In App Store Connect open **Users and Access → Users → +**, enter their name and email, assign **Marketing**, and restrict app access to BeakSpeak. Marketing is one of the roles eligible to be an internal TestFlight tester and can view TestFlight screenshot/crash feedback; Developer access is unnecessary unless they must upload builds, work with technical resources, or use developer-only tooling. For an individual membership, added users get App Store Connect access but are not Apple Developer Program team members and do not receive other membership resources. Apple allows an individual member to add up to 50 such users. [Accounts and roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/overview-of-accounts-and-roles), [Apple Developer Program roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/roles), [TestFlight](https://developer.apple.com/testflight/)

10. **Create an internal group and distribute the processed build.** Under **Apps → BeakSpeak → TestFlight**, create an internal group, add the product owner and bird expert, add the processed build, and optionally enable automatic distribution for subsequent builds. Internal testing supports up to 100 users with Account Holder, Admin, App Manager, Developer, or Marketing roles and does not require TestFlight App Review. Each tester installs Apple's TestFlight app, accepts the email invitation with their Apple Account, and installs BeakSpeak there. [Add testers to builds](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/add-testers-to-builds), [Internal testers definition](https://developer.apple.com/help/glossary/internal-testers/)

11. **Fill in TestFlight information even though the first group is internal.** Go to **TestFlight → Additional → Test Information**. Apple formally requires this information for external testing, not internal-only testing, but issue #31 explicitly requires it. Provide a feedback email and a short beta description such as: “Internal beta of BeakSpeak's Learn, introductory quiz, Review, and Progress flows. Local learning progress is disposable beta data and will not be migrated to the App Store release.” Add any sign-in or review notes if the app later goes to external TestFlight. [Provide test information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/provide-test-information)

12. **Verify installation and built-in diagnostics.** Have both users launch TestFlight, install the same version/build, and complete a short smoke test. App Store Connect's TestFlight tester table reports invitation/install status, device, OS, sessions, crashes, and screenshot feedback without adding a third-party SDK. Testers can submit annotated screenshots and crash context from TestFlight; App Store Connect exposes device and build details, and crash reports remain downloadable for 120 days. Keep symbol upload enabled so Apple can provide symbolicated crash information. [TestFlight tester information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/testflight/testflight-tester-information), [View tester feedback](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/view-tester-feedback), [Beta tester feedback fields](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/testflight/beta-tester-feedback)

## Privacy and release-readiness declarations

- App Store Connect requires a public privacy-policy URL for every iOS app and, for App Store distribution, accurate app-privacy answers covering the app and every integrated third-party partner. If neither the app nor its partners collect data under Apple's definition, select **“No, we do not collect data from this app.”** This must be verified from the shipping code and dependencies rather than inferred from the lack of a backend. [Manage app privacy](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy)
- Confirm whether the current Capacitor version and plugins supply all required privacy manifests/reason declarations. That is a build/dependency audit, not something Apple documentation can determine from the app description alone.
- A support URL, full App Store description, screenshots, category, copyright, age rating, pricing/availability, privacy responses, and other version metadata become necessary for App Store submission. They are not all needed merely to install an internal TestFlight build; do not confuse “internal beta works” with “ready to submit for public review.” [Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information)

## Portal differences and unresolved checks

- Apple changes portal labels and Xcode distribution sheets. Current Xcode documentation shows the preferred preconfigured option as **TestFlight & App Store**; some versions show the equivalent sequence **App Store Connect → Upload**. The decisive check is that the uploaded build is not marked **internal** or **TestFlight Internal Only**.
- Apple's wording is slightly inconsistent for individual accounts: added collaborators are not members of the Apple Developer Program team, while TestFlight documentation calls eligible App Store Connect users “internal testers.” The role and tester documentation nevertheless explicitly includes Marketing. If the portal does not offer the bird expert in the internal group after invitation acceptance, the fallback is an external tester group, which requires TestFlight App Review; record the observed behavior rather than raising their role without evidence.
- App name and bundle-ID availability can only be confirmed inside the activated owner account. Do not treat `com.unformedideas.beakspeak` or “BeakSpeak” as reserved until the portals accept them.
- Tax and banking setup should be deferred unless the first release will be paid or include in-app purchases. The Account Holder should still inspect **Business** for any current agreement that blocks app creation.
- Export-compliance and app-privacy answers are legal/compliance declarations. The likely answers above are implementation-dependent and must be checked against the exact shipping build and its dependencies.
